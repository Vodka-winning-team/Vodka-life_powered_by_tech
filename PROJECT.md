# FraudLens Moris: *Verifye avan ou peye* (check before you pay)

**Read this whole file before you start.** It is the single plan for the team. If you use an AI assistant, give it this file and your section in **Who does what**.

## 1. The app in one paragraph
FraudLens Moris is an offline Android app (Kotlin, no server) that checks a suspicious SMS, WhatsApp or email. It shows:
- **X-Ray:** the warning-sign words, in English, French or Kreol.
- **Official checks:** what official Mauritian records say about the bank, agency or company in the message.
- **What to do now:** the official number to call, a share with someone you trust, and where to report.

The **overseas-job check** is the deepest feature and our lead story. Nothing leaves the phone.

**Hackathon:** Life Powered by Tech, Theme 1 *Powered to Protect*. Deadline **Saturday 14 Nov, 12:30**.

## 2. User and problem
- **User:** Kevin, 24, from Triolet, gets a WhatsApp offer for a hotel job in Canada. The "agency" asks for a Rs 50,000 "processing fee" today, and his mother is asked to lend it.
- **Evidence:**
  - Radio One 2026 reported 20+ alleged victims of a fake recruiter, and Rs 296,000 taken from 4 complainants.
  - The Ministry issued warnings in 2022 and 2023.
  - MAUCORS logged 913 scam reports in 2025.
- **The law:**
  - Private Recruitment Agencies Act 2023, s.5: no agency or employer may charge a worker a recruitment fee.
  - Regulations 2025, reg 7(2): air tickets, police report, passport, visa, processing, work permit and annual fees are paid by the employer.

## 3. Features
| # | Feature | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Scan:** paste a message, or share one in from WhatsApp, SMS or Gmail | MUST |
| 2 | **X-Ray:** highlight tricks (urgency, asking for codes or PIN, payment request, prize or gift, posing as a bank, job fee, guaranteed returns, odd link), with a one-line reason each, in EN/FR/Kreol | MUST |
| 3 | **Bank check:** the message names a bank or payment app (MCB, SBM, Juice, my.t money…) and the link is not its official site → warning, plus the official phone number | MUST |
| 4 | **Job-offer check:** user picks the agency from the register and the cost asked → licence category and validity, plus the rule that the employer pays | MUST |
| 5 | **Result:** exactly 3 levels: **Stop – high risk**, **Be careful – verify**, **No warning signs found – still verify** | MUST |
| 6 | **What to do now:** call the official number (never the one in the message), share a text summary with a trusted person, report (MAUCORS website, bank fraud line, Ministry email `pra@govmu.org` for recruitment) | MUST |
| 7 | **History:** every check saved on the phone | MUST |
| 8 | **Our sources:** each dataset with record count, date and link | MUST |
| 9 | **Job case follow-up:** reopen a job case and add a new demand (e.g. "Rs 15,000 for air ticket") → re-check | SHOULD |
| 10 | **FSC warnings:** website or company matches a Financial Services Commission alert about an unauthorised firm → show the alert date | SHOULD |
| 11 | **CERT-MU campaigns:** X-Ray sign cites the official alert (e.g. Divali WhatsApp cash gift, 30 Oct 2026) | SHOULD |
| 12 | **Shield:** notification when a scam-looking SMS or WhatsApp arrives → "See why" opens the result. No overlay, no call screening | SHOULD (decide at 23:00) |
| 13 | **Languages:** main screens in English, French and Kreol; large text | SHOULD |

**Not building:** AI/ML models, synthetic data, a server, live threat-intelligence lookups, live learning, call screening, quiz.

## 6. Data contract (agree on this first; everyone codes against it)
Every record has `source_url` and `snapshot` (YYYY-MM-DD).

| File | Source | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| `agencies.json` | [Ministry of Labour register, 4 Nov 2026](https://empment-labour.govmu.org/Documents/Recruitment/List%20of%20Recruitment%20Agencies.pdf) (79 licences) | `id, name, category ("abroad" \| "local" \| "non_citizen"), phone, address, valid_from, expiry, page` |
| `fee_rules.json` | [Act 2023](https://empment-labour.govmu.org/SiteAssets/MofedStyles/Doc/Recruitment%20%20Licence/The%20Private%20Recruitment%20Agencies%20Act%202023.pdf) s.5, [Regulations 2025](https://empment-labour.govmu.org/SiteAssets/MofedStyles/Doc/Recruitment%20%20Licence/The%20Private%20Recruitment%20Agencies%20Regulation%202025.pdf) reg 7(2) | `id, cost, words_en, words_fr, words_mfe, payer ("employer"), ref` |
| `banks.json` | [Bank of Mauritius list of licensees](https://www.bom.mu/financial-stability/supervision/licensees/list-of-licensees) + [payment service providers](https://bom.mu/sites/default/files/licensee.pdf) | `id, name, type, keywords, domains, phone, phone_source` |
| `fsc_alerts.json` | [FSC unauthorised entities](https://www.fscmauritius.org/licensing-supervision/unauthorised) (15–25 recent, organisations only) | `id, name, domains, alert_date, alert_url` |
| `cert_alerts.json` | CERT-MU security alerts, e.g. [Divali WhatsApp](https://cert-mu.govmu.org/cert-mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Security-Alert-Divali-Bonus-Scam-on-WhatsApp.pdf), [property fee](https://cert-mu.govmu.org/cert-mu/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Security-Alert-Property-Scam.pdf) | `id, campaign, phrases, alert_date, alert_url` |

bom.mu and the FSC index block scripts, so download those files in a normal browser into `data/raw/`.

**Engine output** (Kotlin, used by the screens):
```kotlin
enum class Level { STOP, CAREFUL, NONE }
data class Source(val name: String, val url: String, val date: String)
data class Finding(val kind: String, val level: Level, val title: String,
                   val detail: String, val highlights: List<IntRange>, val source: Source?)
data class CheckResult(val level: Level, val findings: List<Finding>, val contacts: List<Contact>)
data class Contact(val name: String, val phone: String, val source: Source)
data class JobAnswers(val agencyId: String?, val costId: String?)
fun check(text: String, job: JobAnswers? = null): CheckResult
```
**Level rule:**
- **STOP** if any of: a request for a code or PIN; a link that isn't the official site of the bank named; a job fee the employer should pay; a match with an FSC alert.
- **CAREFUL** if there are other warning signs.
- **NONE** otherwise.

## 9. Demo (about 4 minutes; airplane mode ON throughout)
1. **Job offer:** fictional WhatsApp naming a real licensed agency, "Rs 50,000 processing fee, pay today" → X-Ray → job check: "Listed, jobs abroad, valid. This does not prove the offer is from them." + "Employer pays processing fees, reg 7(2)(e)" → **Stop**.
2. **What to do now:** dialler opens with the register number (don't call); share preview "to Mum" (don't send); case saved.
3. **Follow-up:** reopen the case, add "Rs 15,000 air ticket" → air-ticket rule, reg 7(2)(a).
4. **Bank:** "MCB: verify your account at mcb-login-secure.net" → not MCB's official site (mcb.mu) → MCB's official number → **Stop**.
5. **Honest:** a genuine MCB one-time-code message → **No warning signs found – still verify**.
6. **Our sources** panel.
